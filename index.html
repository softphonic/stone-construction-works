About Us

Crafting Stone Structures That Last Generations

Ashok Jadhav Stone Construction Works is a Maharashtra-based construction firm specialising in natural stone masonry and civil works.

Led by Ashok Jadhav, our team has delivered strong, beautiful and lasting structures for homeowners, farmers, builders and government bodies.

We use only premium-grade basalt and dressed stone, sourced responsibly and laid by experienced masons.

  • Experienced, safety-trained masons
