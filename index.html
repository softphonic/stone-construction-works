About Us

Crafting Stone Structures That Last Generations

Ashok Jadhav Stone Construction Works is a Maharashtra-based construction firm specialising in natural stone masonry and civil works that blend traditional craftsmanship with modern engineering.

Led by Ashok Jadhav, our team has delivered strong, beautiful and lasting structures for homeowners, farmers, builders and government bodies. From rugged stone compound walls to precision nala lining and check dams, we treat every project as a promise.

  • Premium natural stone & basalt
  • Experienced, safety-trained masons
  • Transparent, no-hidden-cost pricing
  • On-time project completion
