FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project.

What areas do you serve?

We take up stone and civil construction projects across Maharashtra, including residential, farm, commercial and government works.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Consultation and site-visit based estimates are completely free and come with transparent, itemised pricing.

What types of stone do you work with?

We primarily work with premium basalt and locally sourced natural stone, including dressed and rubble masonry as per your requirement.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on scope — a compound wall may take a few days, while check dams or nala lining take longer. We share a clear schedule upfront.

Do you handle government and contract works?

Yes, we handle government, gram panchayat and private contract works including nala lining, check dams and retaining walls.

How do I get started?

Simply call or WhatsApp us, or fill the contact form. We will arrange a free consultation and site visit at your convenience.

Do you supply the material as well?

Yes. We can supply premium basalt, rubble and dressed stone along with sand, cement and steel, or work with material supplied by you.

Which payment terms do you follow?

Work is usually billed in stages against measured progress. Terms are agreed in writing before the project starts, with no hidden charges.

Still have a question?

Call us directly and we will answer it straight away — no obligation.
