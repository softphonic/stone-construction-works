Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Last updated: 18 July 2026

Information we collect

This website does not run any analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and it does not store your data on a server. The only information we receive is what you choose to send us: your name, phone number, optional email address, the service you need and your message.

How the enquiry form works

When you submit the enquiry form, your details are used to compose a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send inside WhatsApp. That message is then handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy terms.

How we use your information

We use your contact details only to answer your enquiry, arrange a site visit and share an estimate. We do not sell, rent or share your details with third parties for marketing.

Local storage in your browser

Your chosen language (English or Marathi) is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it on your next visit. It contains no personal data and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.
